File tips: Use a .csv or .txt file with a header row. Expected columns: Date, Product, Quantity, UnitPrice, ExpenseType, ExpenseAmount. Sales rows need a Product, Quantity and UnitPrice. Expense rows leave Product blank and fill in ExpenseType and ExpenseAmount. Dates like 2025-03-14 or 14/03/2025 both work.
